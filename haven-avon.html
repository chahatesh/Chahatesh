Haven Avon Game Jam

On November 15, 2026, I ran my first hackathon. It was a free, one-day game jam for teens at The Loft Events in Hartford.

Finding a venue

I needed a room with Wi-Fi, enough outlets for everyone’s laptops, and space for teams to work all day. I reached out to more than 70 venues, including libraries, makerspaces, museums, and event spaces. The school option was over budget, and other venue leads fell through. The Loft Events in Hartford ended up hosting us.

Raising money and finding prizes

I contacted more than 600 potential sponsors, from local businesses to software companies. We raised over $1,000 to help run the event and lined up over $3,000 in prizes. A lot of the prize value came from software. We also had Steam gift cards, shirts, and custom circuit board badges that I designed.

The outreach totals are rounded down from my email records. I counted repeat emails to the same organization or venue once.

Who supported us

Greylock Games sent a donation, and Elyton donated $50. Our event sponsor list also included CodeCrafters, MatterHackers, PCBWay, Dental Arts of Avon, Universe Sandbox, and Kadmium.

Avon Lions Club mailed a check. It hadn’t arrived when I put this recap together.

What happened on the day

We shared the sign-up link on Instagram and got flyers posted at Costco. Thirty people signed up. I kicked off the day by explaining the rules and what teams could build. We set aside time for workshops and for people to make games on their own or in teams of up to three. By the end of the day, people had made games of their own.

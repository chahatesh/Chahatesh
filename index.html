My Journey

How I went from breaking things to building them.

I was the kid who took apart every remote and circuit board I could find, never putting them back together properly. Now I actually know how to put things back together. Sometimes better than they were before.

I started coding at 8 with Python tutorials and spent weeks crashing programs I thought were bulletproof. I built my first robot for VEX IQ and watched it lose spectacularly in the first competition (burned out a motor on the drive train). But I came back, redesigned the drivetrain, and eventually qualified for state. Twice.

These days I'm building AI assistants, robotics systems, and software platforms that actually solve problems I run into day-to-day. I'm a Stardance Ambassador and mentor other students, mostly because I remember how frustrating it was to Google "why won't my code compile" at midnight. I also spent way too long debugging a servo calibration issue that turned out to be a single misplaced decimal point.

Right now I'm obsessed with making the Atlas AI assistant smarter and figuring out how to integrate it with real robotics hardware—which is harder than it sounds.

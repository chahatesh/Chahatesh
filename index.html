About Me

Student engineer, developer, and maker.

Hi! I'm Chahatesh, a 13-year-old student engineer, developer, and maker passionate about building projects that combine AI, robotics, software, electronics, and mechanical engineering.

I enjoy taking ambitious ideas from concept to reality — designing custom hardware in CAD, programming intelligent software, building embedded systems, and 3D printing functional prototypes. My goal is to create technology that is not only technically impressive but also useful and accessible.

Outside of building projects, I'm a Stardance Ambassador and an active STEM mentor, helping other students learn programming, robotics, CAD, electronics, and engineering. I love sharing what I learn and inspiring others to create.

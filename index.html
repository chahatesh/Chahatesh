Who I am
About Me

I'm a student passionate about building technology that solves real problems. I work across AI, robotics, and embedded hardware — always focused on making something that actually works, not just a demo.


I like projects that combine software and hardware in one system: a robot that has to sense the world and react to it, or an AI that has to run on something you can hold. Along the way I've also built web platforms like StudyPath and Neural Navigator, and I'm teaching myself reinforcement learning by training my own agents from scratch.


I believe the best way to learn is by building. Every project is a chance to solve a real problem and grow as an engineer.

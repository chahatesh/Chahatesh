About

I build software and hardware.

I started and lead an after-school robotics class. I’ve competed in VEX IQ with my school’s team, and I now also compete in VEX V5.

Through VEX, I’ve learned a lot about lift mechanisms, including four-bar, six-bar, double-reverse four-bar (DR4B), and cascade lifts.
